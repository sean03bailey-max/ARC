Settings Guide

  • Deployment Notifications: turn deployment alerts on or off. When enabled, you receive a live alert whenever an administrator posts a new operational dispatch on the ALERTS board.
  • Send Test: fires a sample push notification so you can confirm that local delivery is working on this device.
  • Sound & Theme: toggle UI click sounds and switch between light and dark mode using the utility dock.
