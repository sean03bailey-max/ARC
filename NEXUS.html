How to Use NEXUS

Getting Started

NEXUS is your shared QR-code resource board. Every card holds a QR image, a title, a URL, and an optional rich-text description. All changes sync automatically across all users.

Adding & Managing Resources

  • Click Add Resource to create a new card.
  • Click or drag a QR image onto the dotted square to upload.
  • Click the title or URL to edit them directly on the card.
  • Use the action buttons on each card: ← → reorder, ⎘ copy URL, ⬇ save QR as image, 🗑 delete.

Expanding Cards (Rich Editor)

  • On desktop, click the ⛶ (expand) button on any card to open the full editor.
  • On mobile, tap any resource capsule to expand it in place, then tap ⛶ to open the full editor.
  • Edit the title, URL, and a rich-text description with formatting: Bold, Italic, Underline, and H1/H2 headings.
  • Click Save in the expanded view to persist changes.
  • Drag the ⌗ handle at the modal's bottom-right corner to resize it (desktop).

Board Actions

  • Save Board — exports the entire board as a PNG image.
  • Add Resource — adds a new empty QR card to the board.
  • Navigation to Home, CORE, and TRACKER is handled by the sidebar on the left.

Editing Lock

Click the + button (top-right) and enter the password to unlock editing. When locked, all inputs and actions are disabled but viewing works normally.

QR Blur & Reveal

While editing is locked, QR codes are covered with a white fill to keep their contents private. Click any covered QR (on a card or in the expanded view) to reveal it; click again to cover it back. Each QR toggles independently, and exported images are always sharp.

Keyboard Shortcuts

  • Esc — close any open modal
  • Ctrl+B / I / U — bold / italic / underline in rich editor
  • Enter on card — expand card
